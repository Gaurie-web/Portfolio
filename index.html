    About Me

    Hi, I'm Gaurie 👋
    I'm a software engineer who gets equally excited about fine-tuning an AI model and building an app that recommends music by swiping left or right. Somewhere between "serious engineering" and "fun little side project," that's where I like to live.
    I just finished my Master's in Software Engineering at UT Arlington (undergrad too — I basically never left), and along the way I ended up teaching other students as a TA, which taught me a lot about explaining things without making people's eyes glaze over.
    At eClinicalWorks, I built NLP tools and helped optimize an AI product to hit 90%+ accuracy — but honestly, my favorite part was talking to 15+ healthcare practices and realizing the best ideas come from listening, not guessing.
    I code in Python, JavaScript, React, Java, and a few others, but what I really enjoy is turning an idea into something people actually want to use.
    Currently exploring what's next — let's build something good.
